About Brand

Brand is a demo storefront built as a frontend development internship project with HTML, Tailwind CSS and vanilla JavaScript. The products, suppliers, reviews and prices are sample data, and no real orders are taken.

Shipping

  • Free standard shipping on orders over $100, otherwise a flat $7.99.
  • Estimated delivery: 5 to 10 business days.
  • You can pick your destination country from the “Ship to” menu in the header.

Demo information only.

Money refund

Items can be returned within 30 days in their original condition for a full refund of the item price. Demo policy, no real refunds are processed.
